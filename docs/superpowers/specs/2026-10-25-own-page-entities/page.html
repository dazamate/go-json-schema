How it works

How the moving cost calculator works

You answer the same questions a mover would ask first. The calculator turns them into a realistic price range for your move.

There's no sign-up and no email gate. If the estimate looks right and you want a firm number, you can ask a specialist to confirm it. If not, you leave with a useful number and nothing else.

Price factors

What affects the cost of your move

Six things decide most of what you'll pay. Knowing them helps you read any quote and spot where you can save.

  • Distance

    Local moves are priced by time, long-distance moves mostly by miles and weight. Crossing state lines also brings federal rules into play.

    Big impact

  • How much you're moving

    More rooms means more weight, a bigger truck and more hours. Clearing out before the move is the easiest way to save.

    Big impact

  • Your moving date

    Summer, weekends and month-ends are busiest. A flexible date can lower the price.

    Medium impact

  • Stairs and access

    Flights of stairs, elevators, long walks from the truck or tight streets all add time.

    Medium impact

  • Heavy or delicate items

    Pianos, pool tables, hot tubs and safes need extra crew, special equipment and care.

    Varies

  • Packing and extras

    Packing, storage and car shipping are optional. Add only what you need.

    Optional

From estimate to exact price

Five ways to get a quote you can count on

An estimate tells you what to expect. These steps make sure the final bill matches it.

Based on FMCSA moving guidance

  1. List your big items

    Beds, sofas, appliances and anything heavy. They drive weight, crew size and time.

  2. Ask for a survey

    An in-home or video walk-through gives the mover what they need to firm up the price.

  3. Get it in writing

    Know whether your estimate is binding or non-binding before you sign anything.

  4. Check the USDOT number

    For interstate moves, confirm the mover is registered with the FMCSA.

  5. Choose your coverage

    Decide between basic released value and full value protection before moving day.

Moving cost calculator FAQ

Can't find your answer? Email hello@liftmyboxes.com.

Is this moving cost calculator free?

Yes, completely. There is no charge to use the calculator, see your estimate, or ask a moving specialist for your exact price.

Do I need to enter my email to get an estimate?

No. Your estimate appears before we ask for any contact details. You only share your name, email and phone if you want a specialist to confirm your exact price.

How accurate is a moving cost calculator?

A calculator gives you a realistic starting range, not a final quote. The biggest unknowns are how much you actually own, stairs or long walks at either home, and your exact date. An in-home or video survey turns the range into a firm price.

How do movers price local and long-distance moves?

Local moves are usually charged by the hour for a crew and truck. Long-distance moves are usually priced by the weight or volume of your belongings and the distance, with extras such as packing or stairs added on top.

Does the estimate include packing?

Only if you choose it. Packing, storage and car shipping are optional extras in the calculator, so you can see the price with and without them.

When is the cheapest time to move?

Demand peaks in summer, at weekends and at the start and end of each month, so mid-month weekdays outside May to September are usually the easiest dates to book at a good rate.
